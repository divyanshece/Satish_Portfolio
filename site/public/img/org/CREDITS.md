Commons records the World Bank and Johns Hopkins marks as public domain because
they are below the threshold of originality for copyright. That is a statement
about **copyright**, not about **trademark** — both remain registered marks of
their owners. Showing them to state where he worked is ordinary nominative use,
and it is what an academic CV does; it is not a licence to use them for anything
else. If either institution objects, replace the file with a typographic tile.
